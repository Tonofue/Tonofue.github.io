muse-drive-uni

muse-drive-uni is a private, single-user application operated by Rikiya Tono for his own personal use. It connects his university Google account to his own personal assistant software running on his own computer, so that he can view and manage his own data without opening each Google service manually.

What it does

  • Reads and manages the owner's own Google Drive files (the university account's Drive is secondary storage; primary storage is OneDrive).
  • Reads the owner's university Gmail (read-only; the app cannot send or delete mail).
  • Reads and manages the owner's Google Calendar events, Google Tasks and contacts.
  • Accesses Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Forms files through Drive.

Availability

This application is not offered to the public. There is no sign-up, no login for third parties, and no other user can authorize or use it. Only the owner uses it, with his own Google account.
